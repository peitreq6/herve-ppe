Frequently asked questions

My developer imposes its suppliers. Can you still help?

Yes, that is actually the most common case. We help you make the most of the imposed list, understand the extras and negotiate whatever can be negotiated.

I have already started choosing. Is it too late?

No. Every decision still open counts, and an outside view avoids costly mistakes.

Does the first call commit me to anything?

No. It is free and helps us see whether we can be useful to you. If not, we will tell you.
